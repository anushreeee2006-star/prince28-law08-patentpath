# PatentPath – Requirements

## Functional Requirements

1. The system shall accept an invention description from the user.
2. The system shall identify relevant Section 3 exclusions under the Indian Patents Act, 1970.
3. The system shall explain the difference between provisional and complete patent applications.
4. The system shall provide patent fee category information based on applicant details.
5. The system shall provide relevant patent forms and official source references.

## Non-Functional Requirements

1. The system shall respond to a user query within 5 seconds under normal conditions.
2. The system shall achieve at least 80% agreement with a reviewer on Section 3 flags across 20 invention scenarios.
3. The system shall support at least 50 concurrent users.
4. The system shall maintain at least 99% service availability during the evaluation period.
5. The system shall log every API request with a unique request ID.
